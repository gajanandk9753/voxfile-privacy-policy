VoxFile Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 13, 2026

VoxFile is a Chrome extension that injects user-provided context into AI chat interfaces like ChatGPT and Claude. This Privacy Policy explains what data VoxFile handles and how it is used.

What We Collect

VoxFile collects nothing on our servers. All user data — including profiles, snippets, and preferences — is stored exclusively in your browser's local storage (IndexedDB and chrome.storage.local). This data never leaves your device.

Network Requests We Make

VoxFile makes network requests to two endpoints operated by VoxFile solely for validating Pro subscription licenses:

  • voxfile-license.voxfile.workers.dev/validate
  • voxfile-license.voxfile.workers.dev/create-subscription

These requests transmit only a license key (a random string issued by us) and contain no personal information.

Payments

If you upgrade to VoxFile Pro, payments are processed by Razorpay (razorpay.com). Razorpay handles payment information directly. VoxFile never stores or accesses credit card data, billing addresses, or payment details. VoxFile only receives a non-identifying subscription ID.

Third-Party Services

VoxFile interacts with ChatGPT (chatgpt.com) and Claude (claude.ai) by injecting text you authored into their chat interfaces when you explicitly click the Inject button. VoxFile does not read or store the contents of your conversations.

Cookies and Tracking

VoxFile uses no cookies, analytics, or tracking technology of any kind.

Data Retention

Because VoxFile stores no user data on its servers, there is nothing to retain or delete. Your local data persists in your browser until you remove the extension or clear browser storage.

Your Rights

You have full control over all VoxFile data stored on your device. Use the Export and Import features in settings to back up or migrate your data. Removing the extension deletes all locally stored data.

Children

VoxFile is not directed at children under 13 years of age.

Changes to This Policy

If this Privacy Policy changes materially, the "Last updated" date above will be revised. Continued use of VoxFile after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
